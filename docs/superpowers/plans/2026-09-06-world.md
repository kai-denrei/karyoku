# World Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two generated plates on organic Stålberg terrain, a dirt road found between them, and the MKCX-2 driving from one gate to the other.

**Architecture:** `src/world.js` (pure) composes the organic grid kernel, the noise, two plates and the drive rules into one world with a height function, a road over the quad graph, trees, rocks and a blocking query. `src/drive-rig.js` holds the hull model, keys, tracer pool and cameras shared by the drive and world tabs. `src/world-tab.js` renders and runs it.

**Tech Stack:** as before. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-world-design.md`.

## Global Constraints

As `2026-09-06-plate-generator.md`. Copied kernel modules keep their own conventions (normalised [0,1]² and `vec2.js` pairs) and are scaled at the world boundary only.
